Student Choice

Compare weekly journal activity with Fed settlements and prepare the monthly cover.

Weekly Transaction Journals

Include the prior month's final week. The importer looks for Effective Date, transaction code, Amount, PRIN, CINT, INT, CFEES and LATE.

Fed activity

For reviewed tables, use Date, Direction, Amount and Description. Label a billing debit “Invoice” or use the amount check below.

For a combined Fed workbook, use a Finance provider column to label Student Choice rows.

Billing invoice

The Student Choice PDF supplies the invoice total. For Excel, choose its total cell, or enter the amount manually. If multiple debits share the amount, enter the expected Fed date.

Prepared monthly cover

Use a copy of your monthly cover with its reviewed adjustments. Upload the statement or trial balance below to fill their input cells from the exports.

The tool selects the month on statement tabs with Year and Month columns. Enter the verified totals rows for other reports. It checks the reported ending balances and fills refunds when both the statement and cover have a recognized refunds field.

Draft reconciliation

The filled copy is last month's workbook with this month done: the Cover Page inputs, title and completion details are filled in, and the month's MATCHUP block is added below the previous month's, formatted the same way. It is refused if the Cover does not tie to the provider statement.

The review Excel's MATCHUP sheet is the month's block in the monthly layout (payment groups with their Fed amounts, disbursements, refunds, totals, the invoice line, Transit and the journal-entry table). Copy it from A1 down to the Transit TOTALS row and paste it below the previous month's block. Use the other sheets to review exceptions and source detail.
