LendKey

Compare ACH activity and recalculate statement roll-forwards.

ACH comparison

Provide both exports to compare ACH. Drop the original Fed Detailed Trial Balance, a LendKey-only extract, or a combined workbook with a Finance provider column.

The standard LendKey workbook maps automatically. Rows with missing ACH details appear in source review. The tool checks same-date totals first, then looks for unique batch matches by amount and debit or credit direction across the selected dates.

To use a confirmed posting date in the daily totals, paste the ACH date, ACH direction as reported by LendKey, and Fed posting date. Review carryovers and adjust the date range as needed.

Fed entries whose LendKey date falls in another month (for example ,073126 on an August 3 settlement) are excluded automatically and listed in ACH source review. Use this box only for anything else: paste the Fed worksheet row and the reason for excluding it.

With the Consolidation and Student statement PDFs loaded, Fed credits that add up to each statement's Remittance to Lender are assigned to that product automatically. For anything else, paste the Fed worksheet row and the reason for including it in the reviewed provider total, with the product (Consolidation, Home Improvement or Student) in a third column.

Purpose Matchup and carryovers

The ACH comparison also prepares the Purpose Matchup rows. Enter optional prior carryovers and current statement amounts to include them in the review.

Use positive amounts and leave unavailable figures blank. The workbook lists exact clearing candidates for review.

Principal and interest

Use one PDF per portfolio. The download identifies any lines that still need a reviewed amount.

The provider's Home Improvement portfolio summary fills supported statement cells. Add your prepared monthly workbook to calculate all three product roll-forwards using its remaining inputs.

If the standard provider workbook is loaded above without a Fed file, its portfolio summary is used here automatically.

To fill the Cover Page, also drop the verified Wisdom trial balance. Review the CUSA Entries, recalculate and save the monthly workbook before uploading it.

Draft reconciliation

The filled copy is last month's workbook with this month done: the statement and Wisdom inputs and audit fields are filled in, and the month's Amounts Lineup and Purpose Matchup blocks are added below the previous month's, formatted the same way. Use the review download to check exceptions and source detail.

Review date and direction differences, prior-period rows, and statement variances before copying the draft values to your monthly workbook.

The Amounts Lineup sheet uses the monthly workbook's block layout. Copy from C1 through column T down to the month-end date and paste it below the previous month's block. Unmatched Fed items are listed separately below the block for review. The Purpose Matchup sheet uses the monthly block layout too: copy C1 through column N down to the NEW A/P row and paste it below the previous month. Add the statement PDFs to fill the cleared, in-transit and remittance amounts, and the prepared monthly workbook to carry over the summary's journal entry notes. The Home Improvement late fee is calculated from the provider workbook's ServicingTransactionDetail sheet, the same as its Recon Adj Wkbook AI21 cell, unless you enter one above. Enter Consolidation and Student late fees when they apply.
